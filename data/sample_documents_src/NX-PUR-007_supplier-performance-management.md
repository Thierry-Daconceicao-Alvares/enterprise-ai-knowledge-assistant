## 1. Scope

This procedure applies to all direct material suppliers that delivered to Nexora in the last 12 months.

## 2. Key performance indicators

- **On-time delivery (OTD)**: share of order lines delivered within the agreed window, from 2 days early to 0 days late against the confirmed date. Target: at least 95%.
- **Quality**: defective parts per million (PPM) received. Target: no more than 50 PPM for production parts.
- **Responsiveness**: containment within 24 hours of a quality claim, and an 8D report within 10 business days.
- **Document compliance**: compliance rate as defined in NX-PUR-008. Target: at least 98%.

## 3. Monthly scorecard

Each supplier receives a monthly scorecard with a score from 0 to 100, weighted 40% on delivery, 40% on quality, 10% on responsiveness and 10% on document compliance. Scorecards are published in the supplier portal by the 10th of each month.

## 4. Supplier classification and actions

| Score | Class | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 85 to 100 | A | Preferred supplier, eligible for new business |
| 70 to 84 | B | Improvement plan recommended |
| Below 70 | C | Corrective action plan within 30 days and a monthly review meeting |

A supplier that stays in class C for 3 consecutive months is placed on "new business hold": no new parts or contracts are awarded to it, and the Sourcing Committee evaluates its phase-out.

## 5. Business reviews

Nexora holds quarterly business reviews with its top 20 direct material suppliers by annual spend, covering performance, cost reduction, innovation and risks.

## 6. Quality incidents

A quality incident that stops a customer's production line is escalated immediately to the Head of Supplier Quality. The related costs can be charged back to the supplier under the quality assurance agreement.
