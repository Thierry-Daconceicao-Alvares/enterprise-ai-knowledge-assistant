## 1. Purpose

This document defines the approval limits for purchases at Nexora Industries. Limits apply to the value of each purchase order excluding VAT.

## 2. Approval matrix

| Purchase order value (excluding VAT) | Approver |
|---|---|
| Below €25,000 | Department manager |
| €25,000 to €99,999 | Plant Manager |
| €100,000 to €499,999 | Managing Director |
| €500,000 and above | Group CEO |

## 3. Approval channel

Approvals are given in the ERP workflow or by email. An approval given by email is attached to the purchase order by the buyer.

## 4. Delegation during absence

An approver may delegate their approval authority during an absence for a maximum of 60 calendar days.
