## 1. Purpose

This guide explains how to get help from the Nexora IT Service Desk, how incidents are prioritised and what response times you can expect.

## 2. How to contact the Service Desk

- Nexora Service Portal: available 24 hours a day to open a ticket, follow its progress or order equipment and software from the catalog.
- Phone: internal extension 4357, or +33 4 72 00 43 57 from outside the company network.
- IT corners: walk-in support at the Lyon headquarters and in each plant, from Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 17:00.

The Service Desk is staffed from Monday to Friday, 7:00 to 19:00 (Paris time). Outside these hours, an on-call engineer handles priority 1 incidents only, by phone.

## 3. Priorities and target times

| Priority | Examples | First response | Resolution target |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 Critical | Production line stopped by an IT failure, site-wide outage, security incident | 30 minutes, 24/7 | 4 hours |
| P2 High | A team cannot work and there is no workaround | 2 business hours | 1 business day |
| P3 Medium | One user is affected and a workaround exists | 1 business day | 3 business days |
| P4 Low | Questions and minor issues | 2 business days | 5 business days |

Requests for equipment or software follow the lead times of the service catalog (NX-IT-004), not the incident targets above.

## 4. What to include in a ticket

Give the asset tag of the device (the label under the laptop), the exact error message or a screenshot, the time when the problem started and the number of people affected. A precise ticket is routed to the right team at the first attempt.

## 5. Password resets

Forgotten passwords are reset with the self-service password portal, after verification with multi-factor authentication, as described in NX-IT-002. The Service Desk resets a password by phone only after verifying the caller's identity.

## 6. Escalation

If a ticket is about to miss its resolution target, or if you are not satisfied with the progress, ask the Service Desk to escalate it to the IT Duty Manager. Every closed ticket triggers a short satisfaction survey.
