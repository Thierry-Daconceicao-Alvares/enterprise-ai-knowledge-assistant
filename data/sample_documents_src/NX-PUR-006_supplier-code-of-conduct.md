## 1. Purpose and application

This Code sets the minimum standards that Nexora expects from all its suppliers and their subcontractors. Suppliers sign an acknowledgement of the Code during onboarding and renew it every 3 years.

## 2. Human rights and labour

- Forced labour and child labour are prohibited. The minimum age for employment is 15 years or the local legal minimum age, whichever is higher, and workers under 18 must not perform hazardous work.
- Working hours must not exceed 60 hours per week including overtime, with at least one day off in every seven days, except in emergencies.
- Wages must meet at least the legal minimum. Suppliers respect freedom of association and do not tolerate discrimination or harassment.

## 3. Health and safety

Suppliers provide a safe working environment, carry out risk assessments, train their workers and prepare for emergencies. Any serious accident that occurs while working for Nexora must be reported to Nexora within 48 hours.

## 4. Environment and product compliance

- Suppliers comply with all environmental laws. An environmental management system such as ISO 14001 is recommended.
- Products must comply with the REACH and RoHS regulations where applicable. Suppliers must inform Nexora of any substance of very high concern (SVHC) present above 0.1% weight by weight in the articles they deliver.
- Suppliers work to reduce energy use, emissions and waste. On request, they share their carbon footprint data, and the top suppliers report their Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions every year.

## 5. Business ethics

- Nexora has zero tolerance for bribery and corruption, including facilitation payments.
- Gifts and hospitality offered to Nexora employees must respect NX-ETH-001. No gift of any kind may be offered during a tender.
- Suppliers compete fairly, avoid conflicts of interest and protect confidential information and personal data in line with the GDPR.

## 6. Responsible minerals

Suppliers of parts containing tin, tantalum, tungsten or gold (3TG) or cobalt must source these minerals responsibly, carry out due diligence in line with the OECD Due Diligence Guidance, and report every year using the Conflict Minerals Reporting Template (CMRT), and the Extended Minerals Reporting Template (EMRT) for cobalt.

## 7. Subcontracting

Direct material suppliers may not subcontract the production of parts delivered to Nexora without the prior written approval of Nexora Supplier Quality. Approved subcontractors must also comply with this Code.

## 8. Reporting concerns

Anyone can report a concern confidentially through the Nexora SpeakUp line, by web or by phone, anonymously where the law allows it. Nexora does not tolerate retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.

## 9. Monitoring and consequences

Nexora may audit compliance with this Code with 10 business days' notice, or without notice when a serious breach is suspected. A supplier found in breach must submit a corrective action plan within 30 days. Serious or repeated breaches, such as child labour, forced labour or bribery, can lead to the immediate termination of the business relationship.
