## 1. About this guide

This guide gives practical advice for booking business trips with the Nexora Travel booking tool. It answers the questions that employees ask most often.

## 2. Booking your trip

- Log in to Nexora Travel with your company account. Your profile, cost center and loyalty cards are saved after your first booking.
- Book flights at least 14 days before departure to get the best fares. Train tickets can be booked up to 3 months in advance.
- For trips that take less than 4 hours by train, the booking tool only offers train tickets.
- Hotels shown with a green label in the tool are within the company rate: €180 per night in Paris and €120 in other French cities.

## 3. Changes and emergencies

For a change or a cancellation less than 24 hours before departure, call the travel agency hotline shown in the confirmation email. The hotline is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Unused train tickets must be cancelled in the tool before departure so that they can be refunded.

## 4. Frequently asked questions

- How much can I spend on meals? Breakfast up to €15 when it is not included, lunch up to €25 and dinner up to €30 per person.
- When must I submit my expense report? Within 60 days of your return, in the Nexora Expenses tool, with photos of your receipts.
- Can I travel with a colleague in my car? Yes, car sharing is encouraged. Only the driver claims the mileage.
- Who do I call when I lose my ticket? Call the travel agency hotline; a lost ticket can usually be reissued.
