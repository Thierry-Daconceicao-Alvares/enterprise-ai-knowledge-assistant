## 1. Scope

This chapter of the Employee Handbook applies to all employees of Nexora Industries in France. It summarises the company agreement on working time and the rules for leave. Where the law or the collective agreement of the metallurgy industry is more favourable to the employee, it prevails.

## 2. Working time

- Non-managerial employees work 37 hours per week. The hours above 35 hours are compensated by rest days (RTT), as described in section 4.
- Managers (cadres) work under an annual agreement of 214 working days, without a fixed number of hours, and must respect daily and weekly rest periods.
- Production employees work in shifts. Shift schedules are published at least 4 weeks in advance, and any change is announced at least 7 calendar days in advance, except in emergencies.
- The minimum daily rest is 11 consecutive hours and the minimum weekly rest is 35 consecutive hours.

## 3. Paid leave

- Full-time employees earn 25 working days of paid leave per year, that is 2.08 days per month worked.
- The leave year runs from 1 June to 31 May. Leave that has not been taken by 31 May is lost, unless it could not be taken because of sickness or at the request of the company.
- Each employee takes at least 10 consecutive working days of leave between 1 May and 31 October.
- Leave is requested in MyNexora HR. Requests for more than 5 consecutive days are made at least 4 weeks in advance, and the manager answers within 7 calendar days.
- Plants close for 2 weeks in August and for one week between Christmas and New Year; employees of the plants take part of their leave during these closures.

## 4. Rest days (RTT)

Non-managerial employees on a 37-hour week receive 12 RTT days per year. Managers under the 214-day agreement receive 10 rest days per year. The company may set up to 3 RTT days per year as common bridge days, announced in January. RTT days that have not been taken by 31 December are lost.

## 5. Public holidays

Employees are not expected to work on French public holidays, except in continuous production shifts, where work on a public holiday is paid at double rate. The solidarity day is set on Whit Monday, which is a working day at Nexora.

## 6. Sickness absence

- An employee who is sick informs their manager before the start of their working day or shift, or as soon as possible.
- The medical certificate (arrêt de travail) must be sent within 48 hours, by uploading it in MyNexora HR or by giving it to the HR department.
- Employees with at least one year of service receive a company top-up to their social security sick pay, up to 90% of their salary for the first 30 days of absence.
- After an absence of 30 days or more because of sickness, a return-to-work medical visit with the occupational doctor is organised.

## 7. Special leave for family events

Paid special leave is granted for the following events, on presentation of a supporting document:

- Own wedding or civil partnership (PACS): 4 working days
- Wedding of a child: 1 working day
- Death of a spouse, partner or child: 5 working days
- Death of a parent, brother or sister: 3 working days
- Moving house: 1 working day per year

## 8. Overtime

Overtime must be requested by the line manager in advance and recorded in the time management system. It is paid at the legal rates or, with the employee's agreement, compensated by time off. Managers under the 214-day agreement are not eligible for overtime.

## 9. Time recording

Production and logistics employees clock in and out with their badge at the start and end of each shift. Corrections are approved by the supervisor in the time management system within 7 calendar days.

## 10. Remote work

Remote work is governed by the Remote Work Policy (NX-HR-002).
