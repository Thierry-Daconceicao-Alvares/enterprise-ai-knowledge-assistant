## 1. Purpose

These guidelines explain how to connect securely to Nexora systems when you are not on a Nexora site: at home, at a customer's or supplier's premises, or while travelling.

## 2. Connecting with the VPN

- Internal applications (the ERP, file shares, the quality system) are only reachable through the NexoraConnect VPN client, installed on every Nexora laptop.
- The VPN asks for your password and an MFA approval in the Nexora Authenticator app.
- Email and other cloud applications can be used without the VPN, with MFA.
- Disconnect from the VPN at the end of your working day. Sessions are closed automatically after 12 hours.

## 3. Public Wi-Fi and public places

- Public Wi-Fi networks (hotels, trains, airports, cafes) may be used only with the VPN connected.
- Never use a public or shared computer, such as a hotel business center, to access Nexora systems.
- Use the privacy screen filter provided with your laptop in public places, and never leave your laptop unattended.
- Do not hold confidential conversations in public places.

## 4. Home network

Change the default password of your home router and use WPA2 or WPA3 encryption. Do not connect Nexora laptops to networks shared with unknown devices.

## 5. Lost or stolen devices

Report the loss or theft of a laptop, phone or badge to the Service Desk within 1 hour, by phone at extension 4357, available 24/7 for this purpose. The Service Desk locks the device remotely. For a theft, also file a police report within 24 hours and send a copy to the Service Desk.

## 6. Printing and documents at home

Printing confidential documents at home is not allowed. Paper documents taken home must be stored securely and brought back to the office for destruction in the secure shredding bins.

## 7. Troubleshooting

If the VPN does not connect, check your internet connection, restart the NexoraConnect client and try again. If the problem continues, open a P3 ticket in the Service Portal from your phone, or call the Service Desk if you cannot work at all.
