## 1. Scope

This policy applies to all Nexora employees, and in particular to those who take part in procurement, quality and engineering decisions.

## 2. Gifts from suppliers

- Employees may accept unsolicited gifts of nominal value, up to €50 per supplier per calendar year, such as promotional items or a box of chocolates.
- Cash and cash equivalents (gift cards, vouchers, loans) are never accepted, whatever the amount.
- A gift above €50 must be politely declined. If refusing it would cause offence, it is handed over to the Compliance team.

## 3. Hospitality

- Business meals with a supplier are allowed up to €100 per person, with a maximum of 2 invitations from the same supplier per calendar year.
- Invitations to events such as sports matches or concerts require the prior written approval of the line manager and of Compliance.
- Travel and accommodation for supplier visits, audits and trade fairs are always paid by Nexora, never by a supplier.

## 4. Tender quiet period

From the launch of a tender until the award is communicated, employees involved in the tender must not accept any gift or hospitality, of any value, from the suppliers taking part in it.

## 5. Gift register

Any gift or hospitality that exceeds the limits of sections 2 and 3, whether it was accepted with approval, handed over or refused, must be declared in the gift register within 5 business days.

## 6. Conflicts of interest

- Employees declare their conflicts of interest every year and whenever a new situation arises, for example a family member working for a supplier, a financial interest in a supplier or a secondary activity.
- An employee with a declared conflict of interest must not take part in any decision about the supplier concerned.
- Category Managers of categories classified as high risk by the CPO change category at least every 5 years.

## 7. Breaches

Breaches of this policy may lead to disciplinary action, up to and including dismissal. Questions can be sent to the Compliance team or raised through the SpeakUp line.
