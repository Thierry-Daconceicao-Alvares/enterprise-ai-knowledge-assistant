## 1. Registering visitors

The host registers each visitor in the visitor management system at least one business day in advance. At reception, the visitor shows an identity document and receives a visitor badge, which must be worn visibly and returned when leaving. Visitors are escorted by their host at all times.

## 2. Personal protective equipment

Safety shoes, safety glasses and a high-visibility vest are mandatory for every person who enters a production area, including visitors. Reception lends safety shoes and protective equipment to visitors.

## 3. Photos and confidentiality

Taking photos or videos in production areas is prohibited without the written approval of the Site Director. Visitors may be asked to sign a confidentiality agreement before visiting a production area.

## 4. Contractors

- Every contractor employee completes the site safety induction, a 45-minute session at the HSE office, before starting work. The induction is valid for 12 months.
- A prevention plan signed by Nexora and the contractor is required before any work on site.
- Hazardous work, such as hot work, work at height or work in a confined space, requires a permit to work issued by the Nexora maintenance team for each intervention.
- Work on machines follows the Lockout/Tagout procedure (NX-OPS-002).
- Contractors sign in and out at reception every day.

## 5. Emergencies

In case of alarm, visitors and contractors follow their host or the Nexora person in charge to the assembly point and stay there until the roll call is completed.
