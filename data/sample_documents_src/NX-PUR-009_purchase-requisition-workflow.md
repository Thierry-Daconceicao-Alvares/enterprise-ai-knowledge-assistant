## 1. Purpose

This guide explains, step by step, how to create a purchase requisition (PR) in the ERP and how it is approved and converted into a purchase order (PO). The rules themselves are set by the Global Procurement Policy (NX-PUR-001) and the Delegation of Authority for Purchasing (NX-PUR-002).

## 2. Before you start

- Check whether the item is available in the e-catalog under a frame agreement. If it is, order it from the catalog instead of creating a requisition.
- Check that the supplier has completed onboarding (NX-PUR-003). A requisition cannot be converted into a PO for a supplier that is not active in the vendor master.
- Have the cost center, the budget line and the date when you need the goods or services.

## 3. Step-by-step procedure

1. Create the requisition in the ERP (menu Purchasing > Requisitions > New). Describe the need, the quantity and the estimated total value excluding VAT over the full contract duration, and select the cost center.
2. Attach the sourcing evidence required by section 5 of NX-PUR-001: one quote below €5,000; at least three written quotes from €5,000; from €50,000, also the bid comparison approved by the Category Manager; from €250,000, the award decision of the Sourcing Committee.
3. Submit the requisition. The ERP routes it automatically to the approvers of the matching band of NX-PUR-002. For example, a requisition of €75,000 is sent to the Site Director and then to the Category Manager.
4. Each approver approves or rejects the requisition in the ERP within 3 business days. An automatic reminder is sent after 3 business days, and the requisition is escalated to the approver's line manager after 5 business days without action.
5. Once all approvals are given, the buyer converts the requisition into a purchase order and sends it to the supplier. Only buyers can issue purchase orders, and the supplier may start work only after receiving the PO.

## 4. Changes after approval

If the value of an approved requisition increases by more than 10%, or if the supplier changes, the requisition must be submitted again for approval. A rejected requisition can be corrected and resubmitted; the approvers' comments are kept in its history.

## 5. Tracking and support

You can follow the status of your requisitions in the ERP under "My requisitions". For questions about the workflow, open a request for Procurement Operations in the Nexora Service Portal.
