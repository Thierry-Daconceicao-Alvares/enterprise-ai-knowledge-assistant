## 1. Scope

This policy applies to all user accounts on the Nexora network.

## 2. Password rules

- Passwords must contain at least 8 characters, including an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a digit and a special character.
- Passwords must be changed every 90 days. The system asks you to change your password 7 days before it expires.
- The last 5 passwords cannot be reused.

## 3. Account lockout

An account is locked after 5 failed sign-in attempts and must be unlocked by the IT department.

## 4. Remote access

Multi-factor authentication is required for the VPN.
