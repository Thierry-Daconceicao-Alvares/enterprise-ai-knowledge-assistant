## 1. Standard equipment

- Office employees receive a 14-inch laptop, a docking station, one external monitor and a headset.
- Engineers who use design or simulation software receive a workstation laptop.
- Production teams use shared kiosk computers on the shop floor.
- Mobile phones are provided to employees whose role requires them to be reachable outside the office, with the approval of their line manager.

## 2. Requesting equipment

Equipment is requested in the catalog of the Nexora Service Portal and approved by the line manager. Standard items are delivered within 10 business days after approval. Requests for non-standard equipment must include a business justification and are also approved by the IT Service Manager.

## 3. Software requests

- Software available in the software catalog is pre-approved and can be installed by any employee from the Company Portal app.
- Software that is not in the catalog requires a review by the IT Security team, which takes up to 10 business days, and the approval of the budget owner when a licence must be bought. Free and open-source tools also need this review.
- Standard users do not have administrator rights on their computers. Temporary administrator rights, for a maximum of 24 hours, can be requested through a privileged access request in the Service Portal.

## 4. Replacement cycle

Laptops are replaced every 4 years, or earlier when a device is faulty and cannot be repaired economically. Monitors and docking stations are replaced when they fail.

## 5. Returning equipment

Employees who leave Nexora return all IT equipment, badges and tokens to the IT corner of their site on their last working day. Accounts are disabled at 18:00 on the last working day.

## 6. Personal devices

Nexora email and Teams can be used on a personal smartphone with the Intune app protection installed. Nexora data must never be stored on a personal computer or on a personal cloud storage account.
