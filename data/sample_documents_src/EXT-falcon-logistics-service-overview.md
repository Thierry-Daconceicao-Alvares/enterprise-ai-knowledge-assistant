## About Falcon Logistics

Falcon Logistics SAS is a road freight and warehousing company founded in 2004. It operates 120 trucks and two warehouses, in Lyon and Lille, and serves industrial customers in France and the Benelux countries.

- Cross-docking and storage of pallets in our warehouses.
