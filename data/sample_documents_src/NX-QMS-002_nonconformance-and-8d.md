## 1. Purpose and scope

This procedure describes how nonconforming products are identified, isolated and processed, and how corrective actions are managed with the 8D method. It applies to products from suppliers, products made at Nexora and products returned by customers.

## 2. Identifying and isolating a nonconforming product

- Anyone who detects a nonconforming product stops the operation if necessary, labels the product with a red "HOLD" tag and moves it to the quarantine area of the workshop.
- A nonconformance report (NCR) is raised in the quality system within 24 hours of the detection.
- All suspect products, including those in stock and in transit, are identified and blocked in the ERP.

## 3. Disposition

The Quality Engineer decides on the disposition of the product within 3 business days: use as is, rework, repair, return to the supplier or scrap. Using a nonconforming product as is requires a written derogation from the customer when it affects a customer requirement. A disposition with a value of more than €10,000 is decided by the Material Review Board, which brings together quality, production, engineering and finance.

## 4. The 8D method

An 8D is opened for every customer complaint, every repeated nonconformance and every claim against a supplier. The eight disciplines are:

1. D1 - Build a team with the necessary skills.
2. D2 - Describe the problem precisely (what, where, when, how many).
3. D3 - Contain the problem within 24 hours to protect the customer.
4. D4 - Find the root cause, with methods such as the 5 Whys and the Ishikawa diagram.
5. D5 - Choose permanent corrective actions.
6. D6 - Implement the actions and verify that they work.
7. D7 - Prevent recurrence, by updating the process FMEA, the control plan and the work instructions.
8. D8 - Close the 8D and recognise the team.

Suppliers answer a claim with containment within 24 hours and an 8D report within 10 business days, as required by the Supplier Performance Management procedure (NX-PUR-007).

## 5. Effectiveness check

The 8D is closed only after an effectiveness check, 3 months after the corrective actions were implemented, showing that the problem has not occurred again.

## 6. Escalation

An NCR that is still open after 30 days is escalated to the Plant Quality Manager. An 8D with a corrective action that is late by more than 30 days is reviewed in the monthly quality meeting of the plant.
