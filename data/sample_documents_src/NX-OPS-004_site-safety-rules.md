## 1. General principles

Safety comes before production. Every person on a Nexora site is responsible for their own safety and the safety of others. Anyone can stop a job that they consider dangerous, without fear of sanctions.

## 2. Personal protective equipment

- Employees who work in production areas wear safety shoes and safety glasses at all times, and hearing protection in the zones marked with the hearing protection sign.
- Visitors on an escorted tour who stay on the marked pedestrian walkways are exempt from safety shoes. Safety glasses and a high-visibility vest are mandatory for them.
- Specific equipment, such as cut-resistant gloves or face shields, is defined for each workstation in its work instructions.

## 3. Traffic and forklifts

Pedestrians use the marked walkways and crossings. Forklift drivers hold a valid CACES certificate and an internal driving authorisation, and drive at a maximum of 8 km/h inside buildings. Mobile phones must not be used while walking in production areas.

## 4. Accidents and near misses

Every accident, even minor, is reported immediately to the supervisor. Accidents and near misses are recorded in the HSE system within 24 hours, so that their causes can be analysed and actions taken.

## 5. Emergencies

When the alarm sounds, stop work, leave the building by the nearest emergency exit and go to the assembly point. Do not use lifts and do not go back until the emergency team allows it. Each site organises at least two evacuation drills per year.

## 6. Alcohol, drugs and smoking

Working under the influence of alcohol or drugs is prohibited. Smoking and vaping are allowed only in the designated outdoor areas.
