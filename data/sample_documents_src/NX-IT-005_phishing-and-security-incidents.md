## 1. Recognising phishing

Phishing messages try to make you click a link, open an attachment, approve an MFA request or share information. Warning signs include an unexpected request, urgency, a sender address that does not match the company name, a request to change bank details or a link to a sign-in page.

## 2. Reporting a suspicious email

Use the "Report phishing" button in Outlook. The message is sent to the IT Security team and removed from your mailbox. Do not forward the email to colleagues, do not reply to it and do not click its links.

## 3. If you clicked a link or entered your password

Call the Service Desk immediately at extension 4357. This is handled as a priority 1 security incident. Change your password with the self-service password portal if the Service Desk asks you to, and do not turn off your computer unless asked: the security team may need to analyse it.

## 4. Other security incidents

Report immediately to the Service Desk any suspected malware, data leak, unauthorised access or a document sent to the wrong recipient. Do not try to investigate the incident yourself and do not delete any evidence. A lost or stolen device must be reported within 1 hour (NX-IT-003).

## 5. Phishing simulations

The IT Security team runs simulated phishing campaigns every quarter. Employees who click the link of a simulation are asked to complete a 10-minute training module within 14 days. Results are reported per department, never per person.
