## 1. About Nexora Industries

Nexora Industries designs and manufactures machined and assembled metal components for automotive, energy and industrial machinery customers, and distributes spare parts to maintenance companies. The company has about 1,200 employees. Its headquarters are in Lyon, it operates plants in Lyon, Grenoble and Valenciennes, and a distribution center in Orléans.

## 2. Scope of the quality management system

The quality management system (QMS) covers the design, manufacturing and distribution activities of all sites. All sites are certified to ISO 9001:2015. The Lyon and Valenciennes plants, which supply automotive customers, are also certified to IATF 16949. The certification audits are carried out by an accredited certification body every year.

## 3. Quality policy

Nexora's quality policy commits the company to:

- meet the requirements of its customers and the applicable laws and regulations;
- prevent defects rather than detect them, with a zero-defect mindset;
- improve its processes continuously, based on data and on the ideas of its employees;
- develop long-term partnerships with suppliers that share these standards.

The quality policy is signed by the CEO, displayed on every site and reviewed every year during the management review.

## 4. Process approach

The QMS is organised in three families of processes:

- Management processes: strategy, management review, internal audits and continuous improvement.
- Core processes: quotation and order management, product and process development (APQP), purchasing, production, logistics and after-sales service.
- Support processes: human resources and training, maintenance, metrology, IT and document control.

Each process has an owner, documented procedures and performance indicators.

## 5. Quality objectives and indicators

| Indicator | Target |
|---|---|
| Customer defects (PPM) | Below 20 PPM |
| On-time delivery to customers | At least 97% |
| Supplier defects (PPM) | No more than 50 PPM |
| Cost of poor quality | Below 1.5% of sales |
| Customer complaints answered with an 8D report on time | At least 95% |

The indicators are reviewed every month by each plant management team and every quarter by the Executive Committee.

## 6. Document control

All QMS documents are stored in the document management system, with a document ID, a version number, an owner and an effective date. Each document is reviewed at least every 3 years. When a new version is published, the previous version is archived and marked "Superseded"; it remains available for audit purposes but must not be used. Printed copies are uncontrolled.

## 7. Internal audits

Each process is audited at least once a year according to the annual audit program, and more often when its performance is poor. Internal auditors are trained and certified, and they never audit their own work. Audit findings are recorded in the quality system, and corrective actions are closed within 60 days.

## 8. Management review

The management review takes place twice a year, in March and September, and is chaired by the CEO. It reviews the quality indicators, the results of audits, customer feedback, supplier performance, risks and opportunities, and the resources needed. Its decisions and actions are recorded in minutes.

## 9. Customer complaints

A customer complaint is acknowledged within 24 hours, and containment actions, such as sorting stock at Nexora and at the customer, start within 24 hours. The customer receives an 8D report within 10 business days. Complaints are managed with the nonconformance procedure (NX-QMS-002).

## 10. Records

Quality records are kept for at least 10 years. Records about automotive safety parts, including the production part approval (PPAP) files, are kept for 15 years after the end of production of the part.
