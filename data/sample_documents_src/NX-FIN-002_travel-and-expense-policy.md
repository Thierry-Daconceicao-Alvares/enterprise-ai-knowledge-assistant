## 1. Scope

This policy applies to all employees of Nexora Industries in France who travel for business or incur expenses on behalf of the company. It covers transport, accommodation, meals, mileage and the submission of expense reports. Hospitality offered to or received from suppliers is also subject to the Gifts, Hospitality and Conflicts of Interest policy (NX-ETH-001).

## 2. General principles

- Expenses must be necessary for the business, reasonable and supported by an itemised receipt.
- Employees choose the most economical option that meets the business need, taking travel time and safety into account.
- Personal expenses (for example minibar, personal entertainment, laundry for trips of less than five nights, or traffic fines) are never reimbursed.
- An employee who is unsure whether an expense is allowed asks their line manager before incurring it.

## 3. Booking transport

All trains, flights and rental cars are booked through the Nexora Travel booking tool, which applies the negotiated rates and records the trip for duty-of-care purposes.

- Train is mandatory when the train journey takes less than 4 hours.
- Flights of less than 6 hours are booked in economy class. Flights of 6 hours or more can be booked in premium economy. Business class requires the approval of a member of the Executive Committee.
- Rental cars are booked in the compact category, unless more than three employees travel together.
- Taxis and ride-hailing services are allowed when public transport is not reasonably available, for example late at night or with heavy equipment.

## 4. Accommodation

Hotels are booked through the Nexora Travel booking tool. The maximum rate per night, breakfast included, is €180 in Paris and €120 in other French cities. For international trips, the hotel and meal caps for each country are published by Finance in the Nexora Travel booking tool. A higher rate requires the prior approval of the line manager, for example during a trade fair when no hotel is available under the cap.

## 5. Meals

For business travel in France, meals are reimbursed at actual cost up to the following limits per person, including drinks and tips:

- Breakfast, when it is not included in the hotel rate: €15
- Lunch: €25
- Dinner: €35

Meals with customers or suppliers are paid by the most senior Nexora employee present, who records the names and companies of all participants on the expense report. Business meals with suppliers must also respect the limits of NX-ETH-001.

## 6. Mileage and personal vehicles

An employee may use their personal car for a business trip only when no reasonable public transport alternative exists, and with the approval of their line manager. Mileage is reimbursed according to the official French mileage scale published by Finance each year. Tolls and parking costs are reimbursed with receipts. The employee's personal car insurance must cover business use.

## 7. Expense reports

- Expenses are claimed in the Nexora Expenses tool within 30 calendar days of the date of the expense.
- Each line must have a photo or PDF of the itemised receipt. A credit card slip alone is not a receipt.
- The line manager approves the expense report within 7 calendar days of its submission. Expense reports of more than €5,000 are also approved by the Finance Controller of the entity.
- Approved expenses are reimbursed by bank transfer within 10 business days after approval.
- Expenses claimed more than 90 days after the expense are not reimbursed, except with the approval of the Finance Controller.

## 8. Corporate travel card

Employees who travel more than six times a year can request a corporate travel card. The card may only be used for business expenses covered by this policy. Cash withdrawals are not allowed with the corporate travel card.

## 9. Non-compliance

Expenses that do not comply with this policy are rejected. False or inflated claims are treated as fraud and may lead to disciplinary action.
