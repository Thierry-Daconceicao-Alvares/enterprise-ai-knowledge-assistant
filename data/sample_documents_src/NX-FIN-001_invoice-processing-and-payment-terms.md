## 1. Invoice submission

Suppliers send their invoices as PDF files to the central Accounts Payable mailbox, or through the e-invoicing platform. Each invoice must show a valid Nexora purchase order number, the supplier's VAT number and the delivery or service period. Invoices without a valid PO number are returned to the supplier within 5 business days. Invoices for utilities and statutory fees, which do not require a purchase order, are the only exception: they must show the contract or account reference instead.

## 2. Matching rules

- Goods: three-way match between the purchase order, the goods receipt and the invoice.
- Services: two-way match between the purchase order and the invoice, plus a service entry sheet approved by the requester.
- Price differences follow the tolerance and price block rules of NX-PUR-005. When the invoiced quantity is higher than the quantity received, the invoice is blocked for quantity; the requester resolves a missing goods receipt and the buyer resolves supplier errors.

## 3. Payment terms

- The standard payment term is 60 days net from the invoice date.
- Small and medium-sized suppliers, with annual revenue below €10 million, are paid 30 days net from the invoice date.
- Any other payment term requires the approval of the Category Manager and Treasury and must be written into the contract.
- An early payment discount offered by a supplier is accepted when it is at least 1% for payment within 10 days.

## 4. Payment runs

Payment runs take place twice a week, on Tuesday and Thursday. An urgent payment outside a payment run requires the approval of the Head of Accounts Payable.

## 5. Blocked invoices

Blocked invoices (price block, quantity block, missing goods receipt) are listed in a daily report. Invoices that remain blocked for more than 30 days are reviewed every week by Accounts Payable and Procurement Operations.

## 6. Duplicate invoice prevention

The ERP checks every invoice for duplicates on supplier, invoice number, invoice date and amount. Potential duplicates are held until Accounts Payable has reviewed them.

## 7. Supplier payment inquiries

Suppliers can check the status of their invoices in the supplier portal. Accounts Payable answers supplier payment inquiries within 3 business days.
