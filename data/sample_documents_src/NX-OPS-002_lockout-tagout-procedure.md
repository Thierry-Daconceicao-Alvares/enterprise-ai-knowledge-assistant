## 1. Purpose

This procedure protects people who maintain, repair, clean or unjam machines against the unexpected start-up of the machine or the release of stored energy. It applies to all Nexora employees, temporary workers and contractors.

## 2. Authorised persons

Only authorised persons may apply lockout devices. An authorised person has completed the LOTO training, which is renewed every 2 years (NX-HR-003), and has been appointed in writing by the Maintenance Manager. Each authorised person receives personal red padlocks with a unique key.

## 3. Lockout steps

1. Prepare the intervention: identify all the energy sources of the machine with its LOTO sheet and inform the operators concerned.
2. Stop the machine with its normal controls.
3. Isolate every energy source: electrical, pneumatic, hydraulic, mechanical, thermal and chemical.
4. Apply your personal padlock and a tag with your name and the date on each isolation point. Each person who works on the machine applies their own padlock.
5. Release or block stored energy: bleed the pressure, block the parts that could move by gravity and let hot parts cool down.
6. Verify the absence of energy: try to start the machine with its normal controls and, for electrical energy, check the absence of voltage with a tester.

## 4. Group lockout

When several people work on the same machine, the keys of the isolation padlocks are placed in a group lock box. Each person applies their personal padlock to the box, and the machine can be restarted only when everyone has removed their padlock.

## 5. Removing padlocks

Only the person who applied a padlock may remove it. A padlock left by a person who is absent can be removed only with the written authorisation of the Maintenance Manager, after trying to contact the person and checking that the machine can be restarted safely, using the lock removal form.

## 6. Contractors

Contractors apply this procedure, or their own procedure if it is at least as strict, under the coordination of the Nexora maintenance team. The isolation points are always also locked by a Nexora authorised person.

## 7. Audits

Each site audits the application of this procedure every month, by observing at least two interventions. Any breach of the procedure is treated as a serious safety incident.
