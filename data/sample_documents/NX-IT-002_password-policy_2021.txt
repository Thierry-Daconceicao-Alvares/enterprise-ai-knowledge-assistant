NEXORA INDUSTRIES
Password Policy
Document ID: NX-IT-002 | Version: 1.0 | Effective date: 1 February 2021 | Status: Current
Owner: IT Manager | Classification: Internal
Nexora Industries is a fictional company. This synthetic document was created for a portfolio demonstration and does not describe a real organisation.

1. Scope

This policy applies to all user accounts on the Nexora network.

2. Password rules

- Passwords must contain at least 8 characters, including an uppercase letter, a lowercase
  letter, a digit and a special character.
- Passwords must be changed every 90 days. The system asks you to change your password 7
  days before it expires.
- The last 5 passwords cannot be reused.

3. Account lockout

An account is locked after 5 failed sign-in attempts and must be unlocked by the IT
department.

4. Remote access

Multi-factor authentication is required for the VPN.
