NEXORA INDUSTRIES
New Employee Onboarding Checklist
Document ID: NX-HR-004 | Version: 1.0 | Effective date: 1 May 2025 | Status: Current
Owner: HR Director | Classification: Internal
Nexora Industries is a fictional company. This synthetic document was created for a portfolio demonstration and does not describe a real organisation.

1. Before the first day (manager)

- Order the IT equipment in the Nexora Service Portal at least 10 business days before the
  arrival date, so that it is delivered on time.
- Ask the site reception to prepare the access badge.
- Choose a buddy in the team, who helps the new employee during the first 3 months.
- Plan the first week: meetings with the team and key contacts, and the safety induction.

2. First day

- Welcome by the manager at reception, where the new employee receives the badge.
- Activation of the computer account and set-up of multi-factor authentication with the IT
  corner.
- Safety induction with the site HSE team. It is mandatory before any access to a production
  area.
- Signature of the HR documents that have not been signed electronically.

3. First month

- Complete the mandatory e-learning courses: the code of conduct within 30 days and
  information security (NX-HR-003).
- Visit the main departments of the site.
- Agree on objectives for the trial period with the manager.

4. Trial period and reviews

The trial period lasts 2 months for workers and employees, 3 months for technicians and
supervisors, and 4 months for managers (cadres). It can be renewed once, with the written
agreement of the employee. The manager holds review meetings with the new employee after 30,
60 and 90 days.

5. After 3 months

The employee can ask to work remotely if their role is eligible (NX-HR-002).
