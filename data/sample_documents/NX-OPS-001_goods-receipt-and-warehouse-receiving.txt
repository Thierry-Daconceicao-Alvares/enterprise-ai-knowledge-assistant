NEXORA INDUSTRIES
Goods Receipt and Warehouse Receiving
Document ID: NX-OPS-001 | Version: 2.2 | Effective date: 1 July 2025 | Status: Current
Owner: Head of Logistics | Classification: Internal
Nexora Industries is a fictional company. This synthetic document was created for a portfolio demonstration and does not describe a real organisation.

1. Delivery hours

Plants receive deliveries from Monday to Friday, from 6:00 to 14:00. The Orléans
distribution center receives deliveries from 6:00 to 20:00. Deliveries outside these hours
need an appointment booked by the buyer with the warehouse team.

2. Checks at unloading

- Check that the delivery note shows a valid Nexora purchase order number. Deliveries
  without a PO number are refused, except emergency purchases announced in advance by the
  buyer (NX-PUR-001, section 6).
- Count the packages and compare them with the delivery note.
- Check the packaging for visible damage before signing the carrier's document.

3. Recording the goods receipt

The goods receipt is recorded in the ERP within 24 hours of the delivery, with the quantity
actually received, which may differ from the quantity on the delivery note. Accounts Payable
needs the goods receipt to match and pay the supplier's invoice (NX-FIN-001).

4. Damaged or incorrect deliveries

- Write precise reservations on the carrier's delivery document, for example "3 cartons
  crushed", and take photos.
- Inform the buyer on the same day.
- Confirm the reservations to the carrier by registered letter or email within 3 days, not
  counting public holidays, otherwise the carrier is not liable.

5. Direct materials and incoming inspection

Direct materials and production parts are placed in "quality inspection" stock until Quality
releases them, according to the incoming inspection plan of each part. Parts from a supplier
under a new business hold or in class C (NX-PUR-007) are inspected at 100% until Quality
decides otherwise.

6. Hazardous materials

Hazardous materials are accepted only with their safety data sheet (SDS) in French. They are
stored immediately in the dedicated hazardous materials area, never in the general
warehouse.
