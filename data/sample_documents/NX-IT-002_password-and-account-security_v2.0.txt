NEXORA INDUSTRIES
Password and Account Security Standard
Document ID: NX-IT-002 | Version: 2.0 | Effective date: 1 September 2025 | Status: Current
Owner: Chief Information Security Officer | Classification: Internal
Nexora Industries is a fictional company. This synthetic document was created for a portfolio demonstration and does not describe a real organisation.

1. Scope

This standard applies to every account that gives access to Nexora systems: Windows and
email accounts, the ERP, cloud applications and production systems.

2. Password requirements

- Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Passphrases made of several words are
  encouraged, for example four unrelated words.
- A new password must not be one of your last 10 passwords.
- Passwords are checked automatically against a list of known compromised passwords and are
  refused if they appear in it.
- Never use your Nexora password for a personal account or another website.

3. Password changes

Passwords do not expire and you are not asked to change them periodically. You must change
your password immediately if you think it has been disclosed or compromised, or when the IT
Security team asks you to.

4. Multi-factor authentication

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) with the Nexora Authenticator app is mandatory for email,
the VPN and all cloud applications. Never approve an MFA request that you did not initiate:
report it to the Service Desk as a security incident (NX-IT-005).

5. Account lockout

An account is locked for 15 minutes after 10 consecutive failed sign-in attempts.

6. Resetting a forgotten password

Use the self-service password portal, which verifies your identity with MFA. If you cannot
use the portal, call the Service Desk (extension 4357): the agent verifies your employee ID
and confirms your identity with your line manager before resetting the password.

7. Sharing accounts and passwords

Accounts are personal. Never share your password with anyone, including colleagues or your
manager. The IT department will never ask you for your password, by email, by phone or in
person.

8. Document history

Version 2.0 replaces version 1.0 of February 2021. Main changes: minimum length increased to
14 characters, periodic password changes removed and MFA extended to all cloud applications.
